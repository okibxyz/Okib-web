>Hello

I'm a computer vision researcher with an M.Sc. in Advanced Computing from Morgan State University. I also hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering, with a minor in Environmental Management, from Independent University, Bangladesh.

My research focuses on developing computer vision and deep learning methods for medical image analysis, particularly object detection, segmentation, and visual question answering. I'm interested in building systems that improve medical image understanding and support trustworthy healthcare applications.

Beyond medical AI, I'm also exploring high-performance computing (HPC), with a particular interest in GPU computing, machine learning optimization, and developing efficient, scalable AI systems.
